
# Welcome to the Sigma-Lab @ BJUT!

## 🙋‍♀️ Introduction

Hello! This is the GitHub space for the **Sigma-Lab** led by **Professor [Xiyuan Hu](https://yanzhao.bjut.edu.cn/info/1436/16418.htm)**. We are based at the **School of Computer Science, Beijing University of Technology (BJUT)**.

Our research focuses on **computer vision and deep learning**. We are particularly interested in:
* Adaptive signal separation theory
* Image and video processing
* Multimodal learning


Our team is committed to advancing the performance of deepfake detection models, yielding a series of impactful research findings. These including... [解码可视化特征](项目链接), [多尺度特征](项目链接),[频谱特征](项目链接),[动态图](项目链接),[光照特征](项目链接).
